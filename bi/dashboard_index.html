Metric definitions
  • Net revenue: item value after discounts, excluding cancelled and returned orders (shipping fees excluded).
  • Gross margin: net revenue minus cost of goods at the product's current unit cost.
  • Active customers: customers with at least one kept order in the period.
  • Conversion: sessions with a purchase divided by all sessions.
  • Reconciliation gap: absolute difference between captured payments and order total.
